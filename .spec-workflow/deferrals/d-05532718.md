## Context
On run-20261105-141632 (tradr option-lifecycle-events) the sdd-test-author hit turn-cap twice: task 13 (101 turns, 22 minutes, an 819-line route test file) and task 22 (101 turns, 10 minutes, 9 or more files). Each time the work stayed uncommitted in the worktree, and a retry finished it, because a retry starts a fresh author and nothing resets the tree. TURN_CAP in src/controller/phases/implementation.ts sets sdd-test-author to 100 and sdd-implementer to 150. The retrospective.md and proposals of that spec contain no turn-cap finding: the error waits did not reach the retro inputs.

Third case, 2026-11-07, run-20261106-193640 (tradr ibkr-flex-import): the reviser on requirements v4 hit STEP_TURN_CAP (80, src/controller/phases/document.ts) at 81 turns and 21 minutes. 61 of its calls were Edit, and the last ones trimmed requirements.md a few words at a time under a word cap (3498 words). A retry kept the work, because the document is edited in place.

## Decision Deferred
Decide if the cap for sdd-test-author rises, or if the tasks phase splits wide tasks. Decide if a turn-cap error wait goes into the retro log. Decide if a turn-cap on a step with work in the tree retries once automatically. Decide how a document word cap is met without many small edits, for example one rewrite pass, or a higher cap.
