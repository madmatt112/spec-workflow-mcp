### Integrations

#### Integration
- **Description:** HomePage wires ActionTile's Confirm/Dismiss to useStages().press, which POSTs the controller action route and tracks the pressed/running/done/failed lifecycle; HomePage also retains a tile (via useStages().retained against the live controllerActions snapshot) after the action leaves needsYou, until its stage clears or fails permanently
- **Frontend Component:** HomePage
- **Backend Endpoint:** POST /api/controller/actions/:id/confirm and /dismiss
- **Data Flow:** Confirm/Dismiss click -> useStages.press (local stage: pressed) -> fetch resolves (stage: running or failed) -> controller-actions push updates controllerActions -> useStages.retained advances stage to done/failed against the live action state -> done clears after 5s, failed persists until reload
