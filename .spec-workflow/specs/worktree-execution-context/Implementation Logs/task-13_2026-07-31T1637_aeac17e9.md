#### findOutsideWorkspace
- **Purpose:** Returns the diff pathspecs NOT contained by the workspace. Two containment tests, accepting on either: a lexical one matching the path.resolve spelling resolveLoggedFiles emits (deliberately not realpath-normalized, R4 AC 17), and a realpath one so an entry reaching the workspace through a symlink — accepted by the resolver on its realpath — is not falsely rejected here for its spelling.
