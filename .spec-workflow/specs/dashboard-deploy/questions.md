## Gate A

1. Which runs does a deploy hold?
   Options: Every live run with no operator hold | The three statuses the decomposition names | Those three plus a scheduler-wide stop
   Answer: Every live run with no operator hold

2. What does a press do when there is nothing new to deploy?
   Options: A refusal in the script under the new flag | The full pipeline and a restart | A check before the press
   Answer: A refusal in the script under the new flag

3. What do Pause and Resume do on a run during a deploy?
   Options: Pause moves the hold to the operator and Resume is refused | Resume converts to a deploy hold | Both act as today
   Answer: Pause moves the hold to the operator and Resume is refused

4. Who waits for a live step before the update starts?
   Options: The controller waits and then starts the unit | The unit starts at once and the script waits in place of its refusal
   Answer: The controller waits and then starts the unit

5. Top-level money, personal-data-and-erasure, and legal/compliance posture
   Options: n/a — the spec touches no money, personal data, or legal surface
   Answer: n/a — the spec touches no money, personal data, or legal surface

6. Proceed with this spec, or do nothing and defer it? Reply "Proceed" to continue, or pick "Do nothing — defer this spec" (optionally followed by a reason) to end the run now — the drafted requirements are kept and no design, tasks or implementation run.
   Options: Proceed | Do nothing — defer this spec
   Answer: Proceed
